Accepted Papers

Information for Authors

Camera-Ready Submission

Authors of accepted papers should prepare the camera-ready version following the formatting guidelines described in the Call for Papers.

Presentation

At least one author of each accepted paper must register for ISWC 2026 and present the work at the workshop in Bari, Italy. Presentation slots will be published in the final programme.

Questions?

For any questions regarding accepted papers, please contact us at 5thnlp4kgc@gmail.com.
