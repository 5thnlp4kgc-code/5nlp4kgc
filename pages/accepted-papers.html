Information for Authors

Presentation Format

Each presentation has a 15-minute slot: 12 minutes for the talk, followed by 3 minutes for Q&A. A 30-minute conference break runs from 15:50 to 16:20.

Presentation

At least one author of each accepted paper must register for ISWC 2026 and present the work at the workshop in Bari, Italy, on October 26, 2026. See the workshop programme for the full schedule.

Questions?

For any questions regarding accepted papers, please contact us at 5thnlp4kgc@gmail.com.
