Submission Guidelines

Research Topics

We invite submissions on, but not limited to, the following topics:

Natural Language Understanding
  • Entity extraction and resolution
  • Text entity relationship extraction and linking
Knowledge Graph Construction
  • Ontology learning from text
  • Neural network architectures for knowledge graph construction from text
  • Explainable AI for knowledge graph construction from text
  • Use of LLM agents, tools, and Retrieval Augmented Generation (RAG)
  • Graph RAG approaches combining graph-based methods with RAG
Responsible AI
  • Knowledge Graphs for Bias Mitigation
  • Interpretability and Explainability in Knowledge Graph-driven assessment
  • Responsible AI frameworks for knowledge graph systems
Submission Format
  • Paper Length: Research papers (up to 12 pages), Short papers (up to 6 pages)
  • Format: PDF only
  • Language: English
  • Template: Authors must use the CEURART single-column style by CEUR-WS.
    • CEURART Overleaf Template (LaTeX)
    • CEURART Offline Download (LaTeX & ODT)
    • CEURART Author Guidelines
  • License: All papers must include a Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 (CC BY 4.0) license footnote on the first page.
  • Generative AI: Papers must include a Declaration on Generative AI as required by CEURART.
Proceedings

Proceedings shall be submitted to CEUR-WS.org for online publication as part of joint workshop proceedings co-located with ISWC 2026.

For reference, see an example of joint workshop proceedings from ISWC 2020: CEUR-WS Vol-2722

Submission System

All submissions must be made through EasyChair

Please ensure your paper is properly anonymized and follows the submission guidelines before uploading.

Review Process

Papers will be reviewed by at least 2 members of the program committee. The review process will follow standard double-blind peer review principles.

Acceptance decisions are based on:

  • Technical soundness and novelty
  • Relevance to workshop topics
  • Clarity of presentation
  • Significance of contributions
